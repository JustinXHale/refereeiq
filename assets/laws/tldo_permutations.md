# In-Goal Permutations

This document outlines the different scenarios for when the ball is in-goal, detailing:
1. Who put the ball in-goal & how.
2. Who makes the ball dead & how.
3. The resulting outcome.

## Scenario 1
- Who put the ball in-goal & how: Attackers carry.
- Who makes the ball dead & how: Attackers carry/kick/knock ball TIG or over DBL from in-goal.
- Outcome: 22m DO by the defense.

## Scenario 2
- Who put the ball in-goal & how: Attackers carry.
- Who makes the ball dead & how: Attackers knock forward in-goal.
- Outcome: GLDO by the defense.

## Scenario 3
- Who put the ball in-goal & how: Attackers carry.
- Who makes the ball dead & how: Defenders knock forward in-goal.
- Outcome: 5m Scrum to the attacking team.

## Abbreviations

- 5m Scrum = all Attacking feed
- 22m DO = 22m drop out
- DBL = dead-ball line
- FOP = field-of-play
- GLDO = try line drop out
- HW = half-way line
- TIG = touch-in-goal

## Options 1, 2, 3
- Options 1:22m DO or scrum from where ball was kicked. 
- Options 2:Kick retaken or scrum back at center of restart kick line.
- Options 3:If without delay, kick retaken or scrum at center of restart kick line. If delay, GLDO (implied, but not explicit in Law).

## Other GLDO Scenarios FOP = field-of-play
- Attackers ground ball in goal without infringing = Try. 
- Mark taken by Defense in-goal = FK in line with mark 5m in front of try line.
- Deliberately knocking ball TIG or over DBL = PK.
- knock forward by Attack into in-goal from the field-of-play = Scrum to Defense, no closer than 5m from goal.
- Restart kick crosses sanction line, and then (big wind gust) goes into team's OWN goal and they ground ball or ball goes TIG or over DBL = 5m scrum to opponents.
- If the ball or ball carrier touches the referee or non-player in-goal, Laws 6.11 and 6.12 govern, which could be a try, touchdown, or ball continuing out of play.
- Doubt on which team first grounded ball = 5m scrum, Attacking feed
- Attackers ground ball in goal and simultaneously touch on or over TIG or DBL = 22mDO regardless of how ball got in goal. This law is almost never invoked.
