# 19 Scrum

The purpose of a scrum is to restart play with a contest for possession after a minor infringement or stoppage.

19.1 Where the game is restarted with a scrum and which team throws in is determined as follows:

## Event-Based Scrum Restart Rules

- **Infringement / Stoppage:** A knock forward or throw forward, apart from at a lineout.
  - **Location of Scrum:** In the scrum zone at the point closest to the place of infringement.
  - **Who Throws In:** The non-offending team.

- **Infringement / Stoppage:** A knock forward or throw forward at a lineout; incorrect throw at a lineout; incorrect quick throw.
  - **Location of Scrum:** 15 metres in from the mark of touch.
  - **Who Throws In:** The non-offending team.

- **Infringement / Stoppage:** Offside in open play (scrum option).
  - **Location of Scrum:** In the scrum zone at the point closest to where the offending team last played the ball.
  - **Who Throws In:** The non-offending team.

- **Infringement / Stoppage:** A penalty (scrum option).
  - **Location of Scrum:** In the scrum zone at the point closest to where the infringement took place.
  - **Who Throws In:** The non-offending team.

- **Infringement / Stoppage:** The ball is taken into in-goal by the defending team and made dead.
  - **Location of Scrum:** In the scrum zone at the point closest to where the ball was made dead.
  - **Who Throws In:** The attacking team.

- **Infringement / Stoppage:** An unplayable tackle or ruck.
  - **Location of Scrum:** In the scrum zone at the point closest to where the tackle or ruck took place.
  - **Who Throws In:** The team last moving forward. If neither team was moving forward, the attacking team.

- **Infringement / Stoppage:** A maul that ends unsuccessfully.
  - **Location of Scrum:** In the scrum zone at the point closest to where the maul ended.
  - **Who Throws In:** The team not in possession at the start of the maul. If the referee cannot decide which team had possession, the team moving forward before the maul stopped. If neither team was moving forward, the attacking team.

- **Infringement / Stoppage:** An unplayable maul after a kick in open play.
  - **Location of Scrum:** In the scrum zone at the point nearest to the place of the maul.
  - **Who Throws In:** The team in possession at the start of the maul.

- **Infringement / Stoppage:** An incorrect kick-off or restart kick (scrum option).
  - **Location of Scrum:** In the scrum zone, as near as possible to the middle point of the line behind which the kick-off or restart kick was taken.
  - **Who Throws In:** The non-kicking team.

- **Infringement / Stoppage:** Failure to “use it” at scrum, ruck, or maul.
  - **Location of Scrum:** In the scrum zone at the point closest to where the scrum, ruck, or maul took place.
  - **Who Throws In:** The team not in possession.

- **Infringement / Stoppage:** The ball or ball-carrier touches the referee and either team gains an advantage.
  - **Location of Scrum:** In the scrum zone at the point closest to the incident.
  - **Who Throws In:** The team that last played the ball.

- **Infringement / Stoppage:** Stoppage due to injury.
  - **Location of Scrum:** In the scrum zone at the point where the ball was last played.
  - **Who Throws In:** The team last in possession.

- **Infringement / Stoppage:** Reset scrum – no infringement.
  - **Location of Scrum:** Where the original scrum took place.
  - **Who Throws In:** The team originally awarded the scrum.

- **Infringement / Stoppage:** A penalty attempt at goal not taken within the time limit.
  - **Location of Scrum:** In the scrum zone at the point closest to where the penalty was awarded.
  - **Who Throws In:** The non-offending team.

- **Infringement / Stoppage:** The referee awards a scrum for any other reason not covered in law.
  - **Location of Scrum:** In the scrum zone at the point closest to the place of stoppage.
  - **Who Throws In:** The team that was last moving forward or, if neither team was moving forward, the attacking team.


### Forming a scrum

**Scrum**

19.2 A scrum is formed in the scrum zone at a mark indicated by the referee.

19.3 The referee makes the mark to create the middle line of the scrum, which runs parallel to the try lines.

19.4 Teams must be ready to form the scrum within 30 seconds of the mark being made. Sanction: Free-kick.

19.5 When both teams have 15 players, eight players from each team bind together in formation as outlined in the diagram. Each team must have two props and one hooker in the front row and two locks in the second row. Three back-row players from each team complete the scrum. Sanction: Penalty.

19.6 When a team is reduced to fewer than 15 for any reason, then the number of players in each team in the scrum may be similarly reduced. Where a permitted reduction is made by one team, there is no requirement for the other team to make a similar reduction. However, a team must not have fewer than five players in the scrum.

19.7 The players in the scrum bind in the following way:
- 19.7.a. The props bind to the hooker. Sanction: Penalty.
- 19.7.b. The hooker binds with both arms. This can be either over or under the arms of the props. Sanction: Penalty.
- 19.7.c. The locks bind with the props immediately in front of them and with each other. Sanction: Penalty.
- 19.7.d. All other players in the scrum bind on a lock’s body with at least one arm. Sanction: Penalty.

19.8 The two groups face each other, either side of and parallel to the middle line.

19.9 The two front rows stand not more than an arm’s length apart with the hookers at the mark.

### Engagement

19.10 When both sides are square, stable and stationary, the referee calls “crouch”.
- 19.10.a. The front-rows then adopt a crouched position if they have not already done so. Their heads and shoulders are no lower than their hips, a position that is maintained for the duration of the scrum. Sanction: Free-kick.
- 19.10.b. The front-rows crouch with their heads to the left of their immediate opponents’, so that no player’s head is touching the neck or shoulders of an opponent. Sanction: Free-kick.
- 19.10.c. Hookers must have a 'brake' foot positioned to help stability and to avoid axial loading. Sanction: Free-kick.

19.11 When both sides are square, stable and stationary, the referee calls “bind”.
- 19.11.a. Each loose-head prop binds by placing the left arm inside the right arm of the opposing tight-head prop. Sanction: Penalty.
- 19.11.b. Each tight-head prop binds by placing the right arm outside the left upper arm of the opposing loose-head prop. Sanction: Penalty.
- 19.11.c. Each prop binds by gripping the back or side of their opponent’s jersey. Sanction: Penalty.
- 19.11.d. All players’ binding is maintained for the duration of the scrum. Sanction: Penalty.

19.12 When both sides are square, stable and stationary, with the hooker still applying the brake foot, the referee calls “set”.
- 19.12.a. Only then must the hooker remove the brake foot and the teams engage, completing the formation of the scrum and creating a tunnel into which the ball will be thrown. Sanction: Free-kick.
- 19.12.b. All players must be in position and ready to push forward. Sanction: Free-kick.
- 19.12.c. Each front-row player must have both their feet on the ground, with their weight firmly on at least one foot. Sanction: Free-kick.
- 19.12.d. Each hooker’s feet must be in line with, or behind, the foremost foot of that team’s props. Sanction: Free-kick.

### Throw

19.13 The scrum-half chooses which side of the scrum to throw in the ball.

19.14 The scrum-half holds the ball as shown in the diagram.

19.15 When both sides are square, stable and stationary, the scrum-half throws in the ball:
- 19.15.a. From the chosen side. Sanction: Free-kick.
- 19.15.b. From outside the tunnel. Sanction: Free-kick.
- 19.15.c. Without delay. Sanction: Free-kick.
- 19.15.d. With a single forward movement. Sanction: Free-kick.
- 19.15.e. At a quick speed. Sanction: Free-kick.
- 19.15.f. Straight. The scrum-half may align their shoulder on the middle line of the scrum, thereby standing a shoulder-width closer to their side of the scrum. Sanction: Free-kick.
- 19.15.g. So that it first touches the ground inside the tunnel. Sanction: Free-kick.

### During a scrum

19.16 The scrum begins when the ball leaves the hands of the scrum-half.

19.17 Only when the scrum begins may the teams push. Sanction: Free-kick.

19.18 Possession may be gained by pushing the opposition backwards and off the ball.

19.19 Players may push provided they do so straight and parallel to the ground. Sanction: Penalty.

19.20 Front-row players may gain possession by striking for the ball but only once the ball touches the ground in the tunnel. Sanction: Free-kick.

19.21 A front-row player striking for the ball may do so with either foot but not both at the same time. Sanction: Penalty.
19.22 The hooker from the team which threw in the ball must strike for the ball. Sanction: Free-kick.

19.23 A front-row player must not intentionally kick the ball out of the tunnel from the direction it was thrown. Sanction: Free-kick.

19.24 Any player within the scrum may play the ball but only with their feet or lower legs and they must not lift the ball. Sanction: Penalty.

19.25 If a scrum collapses or if a player in the scrum is lifted or is forced upwards out of the scrum, the referee must blow the whistle immediately so that players stop pushing.

19.26 When the scrum is stationary and the ball has been available at the back of the scrum for three-five seconds, the referee calls “use it”. The team must then play the ball out of the scrum immediately. Sanction: Scrum.

### Offside at a scrum

19.27 Players remain onside for the duration of the scrum. Sanction: Penalty.

19.28 Prior to the start of play in the scrum, the scrum-half of the team not throwing in the ball stands:

a. On that team’s side of the middle line next to the opposing scrum-half, or Sanction: Penalty.

b. At least five metres behind the hindmost foot of their team’s last player in the scrum and remains there until the completion of the scrum. Sanction: Penalty.

19.29 Once play in the scrum begins, the scrum-half of the team in possession has at least one foot level with or behind the ball.

19.30 Once play in the scrum begins, the scrum-half of the team not in possession:

a. Takes up a position near the scrum with both feet no further than the centre line of the tunnel, or Sanction: Penalty.

b. Permanently retires to a point on the offside line either at that team’s hindmost foot, or Sanction: Penalty.

c. Permanently retires at least five metres behind the hindmost foot. Sanction: Penalty.

19.31 All players not participating at the scrum remain at least five metres behind the hindmost foot of their team. Sanction: Penalty.

19.32 When the hindmost foot of a team is in in-goal or within five metres of that team’s try line, the offside line for that team’s non-participants is the try line. Sanction: Penalty.

19.33 As soon as the scrum ends, offside lines no longer apply.

### Resetting a scrum

19.34 When there is no infringement, the referee will stop play and reset the scrum if:
- 19.34.a. The scrum-half throws in the ball and it comes out at either end of the tunnel.
- 19.34.b. The scrum collapses or breaks up before it has otherwise ended.
- 19.34.c. The scrum is wheeled through more than 90 degrees, so the middle line has passed beyond a position parallel to the touchline.
- 19.34.d. Neither side wins possession.
- 19.34.e. The ball is unintentionally kicked out of the tunnel. Exception: If the ball is repeatedly kicked out, the referee must treat this as intentional. Sanction: Penalty.

19.35 When a scrum is reset, the ball is thrown in by the team that previously threw it in.

### Ending a scrum

19.36 The scrum ends:
- 19.36.a. When the ball comes out of the scrum in any direction except the tunnel.
- 19.36.b. When the ball reaches the feet of the hindmost player and it is picked up by that player or is played by that team’s scrum-half.
- 19.36.c. When the number eight picks up the ball from the feet of a second-row player.
- 19.36.d. When the referee blows the whistle for an infringement.
- 19.36.e. When the ball in a scrum is on or over the try line.

### Dangerous play and restricted practices in a scrum

19.37 Dangerous play in a scrum includes:
- 19.37.a. A front-row charging against the opposition. Sanction: Penalty.
- 19.37.b. Pulling an opponent. Sanction: Penalty.
- 19.37.c. Intentionally lifting an opponent off their feet or forcing them upwards out of the scrum. Sanction: Penalty.
- 19.37.d. Intentionally collapsing a scrum. Sanction: Penalty.
- 19.37.e. Intentionally falling or kneeling. Sanction: Penalty.

19.38 Other restricted practices at a scrum include:
- 19.38.a. Falling on or over the ball immediately after it has emerged from the scrum. Sanction: Penalty.
- 19.38.b. Scrum-half kicking the ball while it is in the scrum. Sanction: Penalty.
- 19.38.c. Non-front-row player holding or pushing an opponent. Sanction: Penalty.
- 19.38.d. Bringing the ball back into the scrum once it has left. Sanction: Free-kick.
- 19.38.e. Non-front-row players playing the ball in the tunnel. Sanction: Free-kick.
- 19.38.f. Scrum-half attempting to make an opponent believe the ball is out of the scrum when it is not. Sanction: Free-kick.

### Scrum law variations

19.39 A union may implement the under-19 scrum law variations at defined levels of the game within its jurisdiction.
